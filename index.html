How Jarvis works

From a public notice to a decision, in four steps.

01 · Read

Scrape the register

Every new admission on the IBBI public register, pulled automatically each day.

02 · Match

Map to your book

A rule-based engine matches each target to acquirers in your client base and scores the fit.

03 · Time

Track to the bid

It estimates runway to the Form G / EoI window and sharpens to the exact date once it publishes.

04 · Act

Alert & advise

You're pinged the moment a fit appears — then the desk helps with diligence, 29A and structuring.
